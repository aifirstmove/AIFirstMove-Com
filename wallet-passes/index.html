Wallet passes

A card for your business that lives in your customer's phone

A wallet pass is a digital card saved in Apple Wallet or Google Wallet, next to boarding passes and payment cards. Your customer needs no new app and no password.

What a wallet pass is

You have probably used one already: a boarding pass or a concert ticket saved to your phone. A business can offer the same thing as a loyalty card, a coupon, a membership card or a business card.

A pass is more than a picture of a card. It stays linked to the business that issued it, so the details on it can change after it is saved. A points balance goes up, a coupon is marked as used, a phone number is corrected, and the customer does nothing.

  • Nothing to install. The wallet app is already on the phone. Adding a pass takes one tap.
  • It stays current. When you change the details, the saved pass updates.
  • It can send a short notice. An update can show a brief message on the lock screen.
  • It works at the counter. Each pass carries a code that staff can scan.
  • It works on both kinds of phone. One pass program covers iPhone and Android.

Kinds of wallet pass

Each kind does one job. Most small businesses start with one.

PassWhat it doesA good fit for
Punch cardCounts visits or purchases toward a free item, such as buy nine and the tenth is free.Coffee shops, car washes, barbers
Points cardKeeps a points balance that builds toward a reward.Shops and restaurants with regular customers
CouponHolds one offer or discount that is used once.A launch, a seasonal sale, a welcome offer
Membership cardShows that someone is a member, with their level and benefits. Staff can check members in at the door.Gyms, clubs, associations
Prepaid balanceHolds money or prepaid visits, such as ten classes, and counts down as they are used.Studios, lessons, gift cards
Event ticketAdmits one person to an event and is checked in at the door.Workshops, classes, shows
Business cardHolds your own contact details. Someone scans the code on it and your details are saved to their phone.Owners, sales staff, anyone who networks
Delivery rewardOne pass for each order. It awards points when the delivery is confirmed.Businesses that deliver
Staff IDShows that someone works for you. It is issued privately, never by public sign-up.Teams that visit customers or work on site

Where AI comes in

A pass is a simple first step because it connects to the rest of your follow-up. When someone adds your pass, they can be added to your contact list, and a welcome message can go out without anyone typing it. That is the kind of small, practical job AI and automation do well.
