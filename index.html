Leads and follow-up

How it works

Three steps, starting with your problem

  1. Tell us the problem

    Describe what is slowing your business down, in your own words. You don't have to mention AI at all.

  2. We suggest a first move

    We reply with a practical starting point that fits the tools you already use, explained in plain language.

  3. You decide what's next

    Try it small. If it helps, build on it. If it doesn't, you've learned that without a big commitment.

Questions

Before you get in touch

Do I need to know how AI works?

No. You know your business, and that is the part that matters. Describe the problem and we'll handle the AI side of the conversation.

What if I'm not sure AI can help with my problem?

That's a fine place to start. Tell us about it anyway. Part of a good first move is finding out whether AI is the right tool at all.

What happens after I send a message?

We read it and reply by email. If we need more detail to give you a useful answer, we'll ask.

Is this only for certain kinds of business?

No. If your business has a website, customers and repeated tasks, there is probably somewhere sensible to begin.
