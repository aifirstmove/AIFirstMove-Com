About us

We help businesses make their first move with AI

AI First Move exists for business owners who keep hearing they should be using AI and want a sensible place to begin.

Why "first move"

Most advice about AI starts with the technology. We start with your business. One clear problem, such as slow replies to customers or follow-up that slips, is a better starting point than a long list of tools.

A good first move is small enough to try without disrupting your week and useful enough that you can tell whether it worked.

How we work

  • We start from your problem. You describe it in your own words. You don't need to know anything about AI.
  • We work with what you have. We look first at your website and the business tools you already use.
  • We use plain language. If a technical term is needed, we explain it.
  • We are honest about limits. AI suits some jobs and not others. If it isn't the right fit, we'll say so.

Who is behind AI First Move
